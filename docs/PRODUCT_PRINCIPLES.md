# Second Look Lab - durable product principles
Version: November 2026. Source: independent GPT and Gemini deep-research reports reviewed together. **Principles and hypotheses, not experimentally validated effects for this product.**

## North Star
The core product is an original, surprising, worthwhile interactive experience. A visitor should want the next story **because of curiosity**, not fear of losing XP. Reward discoveries, not indiscriminate clicking.

## Progression
- **The Second Look Dossier** is the canonical product concept. The **Journey** is the underlying model; XP is a quiet historical summary.
- Keep the existing **Core Lab Ranks 0-10** and existing thresholds in v0.5. Do not extend to levels 11-20 until real content and data warrant them.
- Keep **120 XP only for the first completed experience**; replay must not farm XP. Never judge personal moral views as right or wrong.
- Three **Lenses** DECIDE, DETECT, PLAY show **where a person has explored**, not certified intelligence or competence.
- **Collections** should consist of a small coherent set of existing experiences (3-4 to start); optional later Expeditions can group more.
- **Achievements** must reflect actual functionality, not make-believe skills or decisions. Exploration achievements differ from verifiable first-try skill achievements.
- User progress is **not evidence of cognitive improvement or workplace qualifications**.

## Respectful engagement
- No punishing streaks, FOMO countdowns, energy systems, fake peer statistics, global leaderboards or XP purchases.
- Never gate essential security education behind rank or payment.
- A **Focus Mode** hides points and badges but never hides the underlying stories.
- Do not claim specific retention, willingness to pay or conversion effects without real users.
- Avoid assumed psychology from selections when first and second questions have different answer sets.
- Prompts about real science or prevalence figures need sources; fictional stories must be clearly simulated.
- Good core experience: clear setup -> meaningful choice -> consequence -> new evidence -> second look -> satisfying explanation.
- A weekly feature must offer something actually fresh; no fabricated "74% of readers..." claims.

## Privacy and durability
- Browser-first and phone-first. Optional PWA comes before an expensive native app.
- Keep progress event-/fact-derived: store unique completed experience IDs; derive XP, ranks, collection completion and Lenses. Validate imported data and version migrations.
- Preserve v0.4 users and do not silently destroy their existing progress.
- localStorage can be cleared by the visitor or browser, including some Safari ITP cases. Implement **Lab Passport** for voluntary local export/import without an account. A JSON passport is not encrypted and not trustworthy for financial awards.
- No server-side personal data, trackers or paid access until justified and deliberately designed.
- Keep a technology-independent progression engine where reasonable so future PWA/Capacitor can reuse it.

## Scope discipline
Next small increment: Lab Passport + Three Lenses + three *achievable* badges + one real collection + Focus Mode, carefully tested.
Afterwards: installable PWA and mobile UX. Later: better narrative branches, optional opt-in personal archive and richer exploration. Accounts, paid products and global analytics only with an explicit product case.
Automated browser tests validate flow/accessibility basics, **not** desirability or D7/D30 retention. No real-user testing planned yet.
